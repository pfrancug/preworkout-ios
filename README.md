# PreWorkout

**Train smarter. Eat with intent. Everything in one place.**

PreWorkout is a native iPhone app for people who take their training seriously. Log every workout in seconds, explore a large exercise library, dial in your daily calories and macros, and keep your profile and progress together — all in a fast, clean experience built for the gym floor.

- **Log without friction.** Capture sets, reps and weight as you go, so you stay focused on the lift, not the screen.
- **Find the right movement.** Search a rich exercise library by name, muscle or equipment and discover how to progress.
- **Know your numbers.** Get your daily calorie target and a macro split tailored to your body and goals.
- **See the bigger picture.** Your workouts, goals and body data come together in one personal profile.
- **Made for iPhone.** Native, fast and available in English and Polish.

Show up prepared. Leave stronger.
